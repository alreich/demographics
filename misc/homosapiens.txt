Homosapiens

"True humans" first appeared about 2.8 million years ago. By "true human" I mean genus homo, which includes at least a dozen different species: sapien, erectus, neanderthalensis, etc. It does NOT include apes. There were times in the past when multiple human species co-existed. Today, only one human species survives....us, homosapiens. And, since we're the only ones left, some take that as a sign that we're the most successful human species. And, that's fair, but all species are mortal, in that they either go extinct (suddenly) or they evolve (gradually). But they eventually disappear. So, if instead, you measure success by how long the species "survived", that is, died out or evolved, then homoerectus beats homosapiens hands down. Homoerectus walked the earth for almost 2M years before disappearing, while homosapiens has only been around for about 300K years. Of course our story isn't over yet. The 300K estimate is recent, and that estimate keeps creeping upward, slowly, so is probably a bit more, but it's doubtful that it will ever get anywhere near 2M. If we're still around in another 1.7M years, then we can declare victory. (Yay, us!)
